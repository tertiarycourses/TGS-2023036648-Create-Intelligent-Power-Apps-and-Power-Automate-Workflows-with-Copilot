# Staff Expense Claims Policy

**Document owner:** Finance Department
**Version:** 3.2
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Applies to:** All employees and contractors of Contoso Services Singapore Pte Ltd

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy states what business expenses the company reimburses, the claim limits in Singapore dollars, and the deadlines for submission. It applies to expenses paid personally by an employee; anything bought on a company purchasing card or a purchase order is governed by the Procurement Policy instead.

## 2. General principles

- An expense is claimable only if it is **wholly, necessarily and exclusively** incurred for company business.
- Every claim must carry an **itemised tax invoice or receipt**. A credit-card slip, a bank statement line, or a photograph of a hand-written note is not acceptable evidence.
- Expenses are claimed by the person who incurred them. Claiming on behalf of a colleague is not permitted.
- Where an expense is shared, only the claimant's own portion may be claimed.

## 3. Submission deadline

Claims must be submitted through the Finance Self-Service portal within **45 days** of the date on the receipt. Claims older than 45 days are rejected, and an exception requires the written approval of the Finance Director.

## 4. Meals and entertainment

| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Client entertainment (meals, per person) | SGD 120 per person |
| Client entertainment involving alcohol | SGD 150 per person, requires department head approval |
| Team meal (internal, per person) | SGD 45 per person, maximum 4 occasions per team per year |
| Overtime meal (worked past 8:00 pm) | SGD 20 per occasion |

Every entertainment claim must list the names and organisations of all attendees and the business purpose. A claim listing only "client dinner" is returned unapproved.

## 5. Local transport

- Taxi and private-hire fares for business journeys are claimable in full. The claim must state the origin, destination and purpose.
- The daily commute between home and the assigned office is **not claimable**, including on work-from-home days and late nights, unless the employee is recalled to the office after 11:00 pm for an incident.
- Public transport fares are claimable at the actual fare shown on the EZ-Link or SimplyGo statement.
- Mileage for a personal vehicle used on company business is reimbursed at **SGD 0.75 per kilometre**, which is inclusive of fuel, ERP and wear. Parking and ERP charges cannot be claimed separately on top of mileage.
- Season parking at the assigned office is not claimable.

## 6. Mobile, home internet and subscriptions

- A mobile allowance of **SGD 60 per month** is paid to employees in client-facing or on-call roles; itemised mobile bills are not separately claimable.
- Home broadband is **not claimable** — it is covered by the arrangements in the Work-From-Home Policy.
- Professional subscriptions and society memberships are claimable up to **SGD 500 per calendar year** with the department head's prior approval.

## 7. Training and professional development

Course fees, examination fees and materials are claimable where the training is approved in advance by the department head. The company recovers a pro-rated portion of any course costing more than **SGD 2,000** if the employee resigns within 12 months of completion.

## 8. Approval thresholds

| Claim total (per claim) | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to SGD 500 | Reporting manager |
| SGD 501 to SGD 2,000 | Department head |
| SGD 2,001 to SGD 10,000 | Finance Director |
| Above SGD 10,000 | Finance Director and Managing Director |

An approver may not approve their own claim; it routes to the next level up.

## 9. Payment

Approved claims are paid with the following month's salary. The Finance cut-off is the **18th of each month**; a claim approved after the cut-off is paid in the next cycle. Payment is made by GIRO to the employee's registered bank account.

## 10. Non-claimable items

Traffic and parking fines, personal grooming, alcohol consumed alone, personal travel add-ons, hotel minibar, in-flight purchases, loyalty-programme fees, and any expense already reimbursed by a client.

## 11. Misuse

A knowingly false or inflated claim is a disciplinary matter under the Code of Conduct and may result in dismissal and recovery of the amount from final salary.

## 12. Enquiries

Contact finance@contososervices.com.sg or the Finance Business Partner for your department.
