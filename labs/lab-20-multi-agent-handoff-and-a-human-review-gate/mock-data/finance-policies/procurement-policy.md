# Procurement Policy

**Document owner:** Finance Department and Procurement
**Version:** 2.6
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Applies to:** All employees of Contoso Services Singapore Pte Ltd who commit company funds

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how the company buys goods and services, so that spending is competitive, properly authorised, and auditable. No employee may commit the company to a supplier without following it.

## 2. Purchase orders

- A **purchase order (PO) must be raised and approved before** any goods or services are ordered. A supplier invoice received without a matching PO is placed on hold.
- POs are raised in the Finance Self-Service portal and carry a unique number in the format `PO-2026-nnnn`.
- Splitting a single requirement into several smaller orders to stay under an approval threshold is prohibited and is treated as a Code of Conduct breach.

## 3. Quotation thresholds

| Order value (SGD, excl. GST) | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Up to 2,000 | One quotation; reporting manager approval |
| 2,001 to 10,000 | Two written quotations; department head approval |
| 10,001 to 50,000 | Three written quotations; Finance Director approval |
| Above 50,000 | Formal tender with at least three bidders; Finance Director and Managing Director approval |

Where fewer quotations than required are obtainable — for example a sole-source specialist or a proprietary software renewal — a **single-source justification** must be attached to the PO and approved one level above the normal threshold.

## 4. Approved suppliers

- Purchases must be placed with a supplier on the Approved Supplier List wherever one exists for the category.
- A new supplier is onboarded by Procurement after completing the supplier registration form, providing its ACRA business profile, GST registration number and bank details.
- **Bank detail changes are never accepted by email alone.** Procurement must verify the change by calling a previously known contact number. This control exists specifically to prevent invoice-redirection fraud.

## 5. Software and cloud services

- All software, SaaS subscriptions and cloud services require IT and Information Security review before purchase, regardless of value, including free tiers.
- Any service that will process personal data requires a Data Protection Impact Assessment and a data processing agreement signed by Legal.
- Auto-renewing subscriptions must be registered in the contract register so that the renewal date is visible at least 60 days ahead.

## 6. Capital expenditure

Any single asset costing **SGD 5,000 or more** with a useful life beyond one year is capital expenditure. It requires a business case with a payback estimate, Finance Director approval, and is recorded in the fixed asset register. IT hardware is tagged and assigned an owner on delivery.

## 7. Contracts

- Contracts with a total value above **SGD 25,000** or a term longer than 12 months must be reviewed by Legal before signature.
- Only the Managing Director and the Finance Director hold general signing authority. Delegated authority must be in writing and is limited to a stated value.
- Signed contracts are filed in the contract register within 5 working days of execution.

## 8. Goods receipt and invoice payment

1. The requester confirms receipt of goods or acceptance of services in the portal.
2. Finance performs a three-way match of PO, goods receipt and invoice.
3. Standard payment terms are **30 days from invoice date**. Terms shorter than 30 days require Finance Director approval.
4. Discrepancies above SGD 50 are queried with the supplier before payment.

## 9. Purchasing cards

Company purchasing cards are issued to named holders with a default limit of **SGD 3,000 per month**. Card transactions must be reconciled with receipts in the portal within 15 days of the statement date. Cards may not be used for capital expenditure, cash withdrawals or personal purchases, even where the employee intends to repay.

## 10. Emergency purchases

Where a purchase is genuinely urgent — for instance an outage affecting client service — the department head may authorise it verbally, but a retrospective PO with a written justification must be raised within **2 working days**.

## 11. Enquiries

Contact procurement@contososervices.com.sg. The Approved Supplier List and the current quotation templates are published on the Finance intranet.
