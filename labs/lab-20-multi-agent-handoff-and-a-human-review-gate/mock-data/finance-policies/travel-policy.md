# Business Travel Policy

**Document owner:** Finance Department
**Version:** 2.9
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Applies to:** All employees travelling on company business from Singapore

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how business travel is approved, booked and claimed, and the limits that apply. It is designed to keep travellers safe and costs predictable while allowing sensible judgement.

## 2. Approval

- All travel requires an approved Travel Request in the Finance Self-Service portal **before any booking is made**.
- Regional travel within ASEAN requires department head approval. Travel outside ASEAN requires Finance Director approval.
- Travel must be requested at least **10 working days** before departure. Requests inside 10 days need a written reason, as fares are materially higher.
- No more than **three employees from the same team** may travel on the same flight.

## 3. Air travel

| Flight duration | Class |
|---|---|
| Under 5 hours (e.g. Kuala Lumpur, Jakarta, Bangkok, Hong Kong) | Economy |
| 5 to 9 hours (e.g. Tokyo, Sydney, Mumbai) | Economy; Premium Economy with Finance Director approval |
| Over 9 hours (e.g. London, San Francisco) | Premium Economy; Business for the Managing Director only |

- Flights must be booked through the appointed travel management company, which holds the negotiated corporate fares.
- Book the lowest logical fare on a suitable schedule. A fare more than **SGD 250** above the lowest logical option needs a written reason on the request.
- Frequent-flyer miles earned on company travel remain with the employee, but a flight may never be selected for the purpose of earning them.
- Seat selection up to SGD 60 per sector and one checked bag are claimable. Lounge access, upgrades and flexible-fare premiums are not, unless approved in advance.

## 4. Accommodation

Hotels are booked through the travel management company. The nightly caps below are inclusive of taxes and exclusive of breakfast where breakfast is charged separately.

| Destination band | Nightly cap |
|---|---|
| Band A — Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines, Thailand | SGD 200 |
| Band B — Hong Kong, Japan, Australia, South Korea, India, China | SGD 320 |
| Band C — United Kingdom, Western Europe, United States, Middle East | SGD 420 |

Where a conference venue hotel exceeds the cap, the conference rate may be used with department head approval attached to the request.

## 5. Meals and per diem

A per diem replaces individual meal receipts and is paid for each full day away from Singapore:

- Band A: **SGD 70 per day**
- Band B: **SGD 100 per day**
- Band C: **SGD 130 per day**

The per diem is reduced by **30%** for each meal provided by a conference, a client or the hotel rate. Departure and return days are paid at 50%. Alcohol is not covered by the per diem and is claimable only as client entertainment under the Staff Expense Claims Policy.

## 6. Ground transport

Airport transfers, taxis and trains for business purposes are claimable at cost. Car rental requires department head approval and must include collision damage waiver; the traveller must hold a valid licence recognised in that country. Traffic fines are never reimbursed.

## 7. Travel insurance and safety

- All travellers are covered by the corporate travel insurance policy from the moment they leave home until they return. The policy number and the 24-hour assistance number are printed on the approved Travel Request.
- Travel to any destination carrying a Ministry of Foreign Affairs travel advisory requires the Managing Director's written approval.
- Travellers must register their itinerary in the portal so the company can locate staff in an emergency.

## 8. Combining business and personal travel

Personal extensions are permitted where they cost the company nothing more. The employee books the personal portion themselves, and any fare difference is borne by the employee. Annual leave must be applied for in the normal way for the personal days.

## 9. Claiming

Submit the travel claim within **45 days** of returning, attaching the boarding passes, hotel folio and the approved Travel Request number. Foreign currency is converted at the rate on the employee's card statement; where a card was not used, the Monetary Authority of Singapore mid-rate on the transaction date applies.
