# Medical and Healthcare Benefits Policy

**Document owner:** Human Resources Department
**Version:** 2.4
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Applies to:** All confirmed permanent employees of Contoso Services Singapore Pte Ltd

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the medical, dental, hospitalisation and wellness benefits provided by the company, the annual claim caps in Singapore dollars, and the process for submitting a claim. Contract staff on engagements shorter than 12 months are covered by sections 4 and 5 only.

## 2. Outpatient medical benefit

- Each confirmed employee may claim up to **SGD 800 per calendar year** for outpatient consultations, prescribed medication and diagnostic tests.
- Claims may be made at any registered general practitioner, polyclinic or specialist clinic in Singapore.
- A **specialist consultation requires a referral letter** from a general practitioner. Without a referral, the specialist claim is capped at SGD 120 per visit.
- Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) consultations at a registered TCM practitioner are claimable within the same SGD 800 cap, subject to a sub-limit of **SGD 300 per calendar year**.
- Unused outpatient balance is not carried forward and is not encashable.

## 3. Dental benefit

- The dental benefit is **SGD 300 per calendar year** per employee.
- Covered: scaling and polishing, fillings, extractions, X-rays and treatment of gum disease.
- Not covered: cosmetic dentistry, teeth whitening, veneers, orthodontic braces and implants.
- Dental claims are separate from and additional to the outpatient cap in section 2.

## 4. Hospitalisation and surgical insurance

- All employees are enrolled in the company group hospitalisation and surgical plan from their first day of employment.
- The plan covers up to **SGD 150,000 per policy year** on an as-charged basis for ward class B1 and above in restructured hospitals, and equivalent private hospital wards.
- A **co-insurance of 10%** applies to private hospital admissions, capped at SGD 3,000 per admission.
- Pre-existing conditions declared at enrolment are covered after a **12-month waiting period**.
- Employees may extend the plan to a spouse and children at their own cost through the annual enrolment window in November.

## 5. Group personal accident insurance

Coverage is **24 times the employee's monthly basic salary**, subject to a maximum of SGD 500,000, for accidental death or permanent disability. Cover applies worldwide, both during and outside working hours.

## 6. Wellness allowance

- A **SGD 400 per calendar year** wellness allowance is available to each confirmed employee.
- Claimable items: gym and fitness studio memberships, sports equipment, health screening packages beyond the company-funded screening, vaccinations, smoking-cessation programmes and mental-wellness subscriptions.
- Not claimable: supplements, cosmetic treatments, spa and massage services, and sports apparel.

## 7. Annual health screening

Employees aged 40 and above are entitled to a company-funded health screening once per calendar year at a panel provider. Employees below 40 may claim a screening against the wellness allowance in section 6.

## 8. Submitting a claim

1. Submit the claim through the HR Self-Service portal within **60 days** of the date on the receipt. Claims submitted after 60 days are rejected without exception.
2. Attach a clear image of the original itemised receipt showing the clinic name, date, patient name and amount. Credit card slips alone are not accepted.
3. Claims are processed in the payroll cycle following approval. The payroll cut-off is the **18th of each month**.
4. Reimbursement is paid together with salary into the employee's registered bank account.
5. Where a claim is rejected, the reason is stated in the portal and the employee may resubmit with additional documentation within 14 days.

## 9. Exclusions

The following are excluded from all benefits in this policy: cosmetic and aesthetic procedures, refractive eye surgery including LASIK, fertility treatment, elective procedures performed overseas without prior approval, and any treatment arising from a self-inflicted injury.

Contact benefits@contososervices.com.sg. Insurance policy documents are published on the HR intranet under Benefits.
