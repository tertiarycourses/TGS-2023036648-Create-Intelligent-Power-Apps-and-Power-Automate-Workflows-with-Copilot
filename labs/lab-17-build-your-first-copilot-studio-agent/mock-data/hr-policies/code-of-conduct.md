# Code of Conduct

**Document owner:** Human Resources Department and Legal
**Version:** 4.0
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, interns and directors of Contoso Services Singapore Pte Ltd

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This Code sets the standard of behaviour expected of everyone who works for or on behalf of the company. It applies at the workplace, at client sites, at company events, and in any online forum where an individual can be identified as representing the company.

## 2. Professional conduct

Employees are expected to:

- Treat colleagues, clients, suppliers and members of the public with courtesy and respect.
- Deliver work honestly and to the agreed standard, and raise problems early rather than concealing them.
- Comply with all applicable Singapore laws and with company policies, including this Code.
- Use company time, equipment and funds for company purposes.

## 3. Anti-harassment and anti-discrimination

The company does not tolerate harassment or discrimination on the grounds of race, religion, nationality, gender, age, marital or family status, or disability, consistent with the Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices and the Protection from Harassment Act.

Harassment includes unwelcome physical contact, offensive remarks or images, repeated unwanted attention, and the threatening or abusive use of email, chat or social media. Any employee who experiences or witnesses such behaviour should report it under section 8.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

An employee must declare in writing to their department head any circumstance that could reasonably be seen to compromise their objectivity, including:

- Directorship, employment or a shareholding above **5%** in a supplier, client or competitor.
- A close personal or family relationship with a person involved in a procurement decision, an interview panel or a performance review.
- Any outside employment or paid consultancy. Outside work requires the prior written approval of the department head and must not compete with the company or be performed during working hours.

Declarations are made through the HR Self-Service portal and are reviewed annually.

## 5. Gifts, entertainment and anti-bribery

- Offering or accepting any bribe, kickback or improper payment is strictly prohibited and may be a criminal offence under the Prevention of Corruption Act.
- A gift with a value of **SGD 100 or less** may be accepted without approval, provided it is not cash or a cash equivalent.
- A gift with a value **above SGD 100** must be declared to the department head within 5 working days and is normally either returned, donated, or shared with the wider team.
- Cash, vouchers, loans and personal discounts may never be accepted, at any value.
- Reasonable business meals and hospitality are acceptable where they are infrequent, modest and connected to a genuine business discussion. Anything above **SGD 200 per person** requires prior approval.

## 6. Confidentiality and personal data

- Company and client confidential information must not be disclosed to any third party during or after employment.
- Personal data must be collected, used and disclosed only for the purposes consented to, in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012.
- Personal data may not be exported to personal accounts, personal devices or unapproved AI services.
- A suspected data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer at dpo@contososervices.com.sg **within 24 hours** of discovery.

## 7. Use of company systems and AI tools

Company email, chat and internet access are provided for business use. Limited personal use is permitted where it does not interfere with work. Employees may use only the generative AI services approved on the IT service catalogue, and must not enter client data, personal data or unpublished financial information into any unapproved AI tool.

## 8. Reporting a concern

Concerns may be raised with the reporting manager, the HR Business Partner, or confidentially through the whistleblowing channel at speakup@contososervices.com.sg, which is monitored by the Audit Committee. Reports may be made anonymously. The company prohibits retaliation of any kind against a person who raises a concern in good faith; retaliation is itself a disciplinary offence.

## 9. Breach of this Code

A breach may result in disciplinary action up to and including summary dismissal, and where the law has been broken, referral to the relevant Singapore authority. Investigations follow the company's disciplinary procedure, which provides the employee an opportunity to respond before any decision is taken.

## 10. Acknowledgement

Every employee confirms in the HR Self-Service portal that they have read and understood this Code within 14 days of joining and again at each annual refresh.
