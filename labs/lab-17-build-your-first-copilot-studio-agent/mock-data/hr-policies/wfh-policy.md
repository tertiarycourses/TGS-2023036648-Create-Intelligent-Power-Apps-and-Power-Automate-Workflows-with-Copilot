# Flexible and Work-From-Home Policy

**Document owner:** Human Resources Department
**Version:** 2.3
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Applies to:** All employees of Contoso Services Singapore Pte Ltd whose role can be performed remotely

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the company's flexible work arrangements, including working from home. It is aligned with the Tripartite Guidelines on Flexible Work Arrangement Requests that took effect in Singapore on 1 December 2024.

## 2. Standard arrangement

- Employees in eligible roles may work from home **up to 2 days per week**, with the reporting manager's approval.
- The remaining **3 days per week must be worked at the assigned office location**. Teams are expected to align on at least one common in-office anchor day per week.
- Work-from-home days are agreed as a recurring pattern with the manager and recorded in the HR Self-Service portal. Ad-hoc changes require the manager's approval by 5:00 pm on the preceding working day.

## 3. Eligibility

An employee is eligible if all of the following apply:

1. The employee has completed the 6-month probation period.
2. The role does not require physical presence — front-desk, facilities, laboratory and customer-site roles are excluded.
3. The employee's last performance rating was **Meets Expectations or better**.
4. The employee has a suitable home working environment as described in section 6.

## 4. Requesting a different arrangement

Employees may formally request a flexible work arrangement that differs from the standard — for example a compressed work week, staggered hours or additional remote days.

1. Submit the request in writing through the HR Self-Service portal, stating the arrangement sought, the proposed start date and the expected duration.
2. The reporting manager must discuss the request with the employee and respond **in writing within 2 months** of receiving it.
3. If a request is declined, the manager must state a business-related reason, such as cost, effect on productivity or output, feasibility of reallocating work, or a detrimental effect on customer service.
4. An employee whose request is declined may submit a fresh request after **6 months**, or earlier if circumstances change materially.

## 5. Working hours and availability

- Core hours are **10:00 am to 4:00 pm Singapore time**. Employees must be contactable and available for meetings during core hours regardless of location.
- Standard working hours are 42 per week. Start times may be staggered between 8:00 am and 10:00 am with manager agreement.
- Employees must update their Microsoft Teams status and shared calendar to reflect their working location each day.
- There is no expectation to respond to messages outside working hours other than during a declared incident.

## 6. Home working environment

Employees working from home are responsible for maintaining:

- A broadband connection of at least **50 Mbps download**.
- A quiet space suitable for confidential calls.
- A safe and ergonomic setup. A **one-time SGD 300 home-office allowance** is available to eligible employees to purchase a chair, monitor riser, keyboard or desk lamp. The allowance is claimed once per employee, not once per year.

## 7. Equipment and security

- Company-issued laptops must be used for all work. Personal devices may not be used to access company data.
- Devices must remain enrolled in Microsoft Intune with disk encryption and automatic screen lock enabled.
- Company data may not be stored on personal cloud storage. Use OneDrive for Business or the approved SharePoint sites.
- Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the company VPN active.
- Any suspected data loss or device theft must be reported to ithelpdesk@contososervices.com.sg **within 24 hours**.

## 8. Overseas remote working

Working from outside Singapore is **not permitted** under this policy without prior written approval from the department head and the HR Director, and is limited to **14 calendar days per year**, owing to tax residency, immigration and data-residency obligations.

## 9. Review and withdrawal

Arrangements are reviewed at each half-yearly performance conversation. The company may withdraw an individual arrangement, giving **4 weeks' notice**, where business needs change or where the arrangement is no longer working. Withdrawal is not a disciplinary measure and does not affect the employee's terms of employment.

## 10. Enquiries

Contact your HR Business Partner or hr@contososervices.com.sg.
