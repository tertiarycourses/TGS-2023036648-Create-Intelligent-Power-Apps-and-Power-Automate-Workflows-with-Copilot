# Annual Leave and Notice Policy

**Document owner:** Human Resources Department
**Version:** 3.1
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Applies to:** All permanent and contract employees of Contoso Services Singapore Pte Ltd

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the annual leave entitlement, application process, carry-forward rules and notice periods for employees based in Singapore. It is written to meet or exceed the minimum standards of the Employment Act (Cap. 91) and the Tripartite Guidelines issued by the Ministry of Manpower.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement

Entitlement is based on completed years of continuous service as at 1 January of each calendar year.

| Completed years of service | Annual leave days per calendar year |
|---|---|
| Less than 1 year (first year) | 14 days |
| 1 to 2 years | 16 days |
| 3 to 4 years | 18 days |
| 5 to 7 years | 20 days |
| 8 years and above | 22 days |

Employees who join part-way through a calendar year receive a pro-rated entitlement, calculated as:

> (Completed months of service in the year ÷ 12) × annual entitlement, rounded up to the nearest half day.

An employee who joins on 1 July with a 14-day entitlement therefore receives 7 days for that first calendar year.

## 3. Applying for leave

1. All leave is applied for through the HR Self-Service portal, not by email or verbal agreement.
2. Applications of **3 consecutive days or fewer** require at least **3 working days'** notice.
3. Applications of **4 consecutive days or more** require at least **10 working days'** notice.
4. The reporting manager must approve or decline within 3 working days. An application that is neither approved nor declined within 5 working days is escalated automatically to the department head.
5. Leave taken without an approved application is treated as unauthorised absence and is unpaid.

## 4. Carry forward and encashment

- An employee may carry forward a maximum of **7 unused annual leave days** into the following calendar year.
- Carried-forward days must be consumed by **31 March** of the following year. Any balance remaining on 1 April is forfeited without compensation.
- Annual leave is **not encashable** during employment. On resignation or termination, untaken earned leave is paid out at the employee's gross daily rate of pay.
- The gross daily rate is calculated as: (12 × monthly gross salary) ÷ (52 × 5).

## 5. Leave during the notice period

Annual leave may be taken during the notice period only with the written agreement of the reporting manager. The company may, at its discretion, offset untaken leave against the notice period rather than paying it out.

## 6. Notice periods

The notice period applies equally to resignation by the employee and to termination by the company, except where termination is for misconduct.

| Completed years of service | Notice period |
|---|---|
| Less than 6 months (probation) | 1 week |
| 6 months to less than 2 years | 1 month |
| 2 years to less than 5 years | 2 months |
| 5 years and above | 3 months |

Either party may shorten the notice period by paying salary in lieu for the unserved portion, calculated at the gross daily rate defined in section 4.

## 7. Other leave types

- **Sick leave:** 14 days outpatient and 60 days hospitalisation per calendar year, subject to a medical certificate from a registered practitioner.
- **Maternity leave:** 16 weeks for eligible employees, in line with the Child Development Co-Savings Act.
- **Paternity leave:** 4 weeks for eligible fathers of Singapore Citizen children.
- **Childcare leave:** 6 days per calendar year for employees with a child under 7 years old.
- **Unpaid leave:** granted at the sole discretion of the department head and the HR Director, normally capped at 20 working days in a calendar year.

## 8. Public holidays

Employees are entitled to the 11 gazetted public holidays in Singapore. Where a public holiday falls on a Sunday or a non-working day, the following working day is granted as a holiday in lieu.

## 9. Enquiries

Questions about this policy should be directed to hr@contososervices.com.sg or to the HR Business Partner assigned to your department. This policy is reviewed annually by the HR Director.
